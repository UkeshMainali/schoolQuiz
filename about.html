About

Quizzes made simple for the classroom

Students type their name once and see the quizzes for their grade. Teachers grade, review, print and follow each student's progress from one dashboard.

For students

One question at a time, answers saved as you go, and a clear list of what is done.

For teachers

Grading, review, student history, analysis and reports that print as one PDF.

Private by design

Answer keys and other students' results are never sent to student devices.
